## #2286 - Support 5-axis and rotary operations in aligned setups

**Chosen:** Route `selection::drive_chains` (threaded from `operation::material_region::drive_chains_of`) through `setup_align::pose_in`, route `fixture::rotary_axis_of` through `setup_align::facets_in`, add all ten 5-axis and rotary kinds (`Contour5D`, `Parallel5D`, `Swarf`, `AdvancedSwarf`, `RotaryContour`, `RotaryParallel`, `RotaryOffset`, `MultiAxisRough`, `Flow5D`, `MultiAxisContour`) to `setup_align::verified_kind`, and update `generation_problem` and module docs. Pin every kind in `crates/app/tests/setup_align_multiaxis.rs` (two-axis `[90, 0, 90]` and oblique `[35, 25, 40]` turns): turned setup matches baked mesh across toolpath (`from`, `to`, `axis`), rotary axis, fixed workholding clearance, and posted G-code, while the as-modelled part cuts differently or refuses.

**Validation:** On unchanged `upstream/main`, every multiaxis/rotary kind refused at `generation_problem`. Independent mutations reverting `selection::drive_chains` (`model::transform_of`), `fixture::rotary_axis_of` (`model::world_facets`), and `setup_align::verified_kind` failed their pinning tests (`picked chain 1 stands at z -5.000`, `RotaryParallel: axis [0.0, 0.0, 1.0]`, and `9 failed`) and passed upon restoration. `Probe` and non-mill setups stay refused.

**Why:** All ten kinds consume setup-frame `material.facets`, `part_aabb_in` heights, and `stock`/`fixture` furniture, leaving only two unaligned world reads: `MultiAxisContour` picked curves (`selection::drive_chains`) and rotary axis fitting for workholding, `fixture_struck`, and `stock_sim_rotary` (`fixture::rotary_axis_of`). Routing both through `setup_align` gives one source of truth across generation, verification, workholding, and rotary chord planning.

**Dropped:** Post-transforming `drive_chains` outputs in `generate.rs` or adjusting `multiaxis::axis::fit` separately was dropped because downstream callers of `fixture::rotary_axis_of` (`fixture_struck`, `stock_sim_rotary`, `job::workholding`) would remain inconsistent and any second transform risks double-rotating an unaligned model.

**Cost to change later:** Any future curve selection or rotary-axis world reader must use `setup_align::{pose_in, facets_in}` and extend `setup_align_multiaxis.rs`; any individual kind can be gated off in `verified_kind` in one line.
