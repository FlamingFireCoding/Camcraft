## #3077 - The first traverse of a cross drill on a lathe or a mill-turn

- **What was decided** (issue worker, unattended; the maintainer can change it):
  1. **The premise is the maintainer's (#2987, #2997).** The unknown start is the top of the machine's travel: on a lathe the turret's index position (the tool-change position), at the top of the X and Z travel. This is the same premise as the upright law and the 3+2 table law. NOT covered: a program that starts with the tool low near the work, for example after a manual jog.
  2. **The lathe post's real first move is judged.** `fanuc-lathe.cps` `liveMove` writes `G00 C` with the turret at the index position, then `G00 X Z` to the first point of the section. A G00 moves each axis at its own rate (RS-274 / ISO 6983), so the tip can pass any point of the box between the index position and the first point: every radius from the first point's radius out, at every height from its Z up. The tool body stands outward from the tip, so the band of heights starts one tool radius (the drill or a wider shank) under the tip.
  3. **The law** (`camcraft_sim::verify::live_first_traverse`). The swept solid is a slab of the part frame, about the spindle line through the work offset: `u >= r`, `|v| <= tool radius`, `z >= z - tool radius` (`u` the first point's radial direction, `v` across it). Both ends of the first move are judged.
     - Work and turning steel stand at the C angle the post states before X and Z move, so they are read in the slab exactly: each facet (a box as its twelve facets) is clipped by the slab's four planes. Work: the source (the part, or the stock on a stock source), the kept parts, the neighbour parts, and the setup's stock (its facets, or its box when it has no facets). Turning steel: the chuck (`fixture::workholding_for` `.0`).
     - Fixed steel (a tailstock, the machine table) stands in the machine frame at an angle the part frame does not state. It is read revolved about the spindle: its largest radius (the four X/Y corners) when its top reaches the band's lowest height. That is stricter than its one angle, and it holds at any C.
  4. **A refusal by name, not a warning.** The cross branch of generation refuses the drill (`GenError::Invalid`): "cross drill: the first rapid comes from the turret's index position ...". No toolpath, so nothing posts. A start that cannot be read (not finite) is refused by name too.
  5. **A mill-turn answers to two laws.** Its default post is the mill post (`posting::default_post_for`: `Kind::MillTurn` gives the builtin Fanuc mill post). That post sends Z to the clearance height, then X/Y from an unknown X/Y, then the full block with the rotary words (decision #2965 item 1). So a mill-turn cross drill also answers to the upright law (`first_traverse::judge` with the cross drill's lifted start). The live-tool law also judges it: the last block goes down to the first point at its radius, inside the same band. A mill-turn that posts through a lathe post is judged by both laws, which is stricter than its own post.
  6. **Cache.** The cross drill's op stamp takes one more word, `live-traverse-3077`. Such a cache reads Stale once and the post refuses it until a regenerate.
- **Why.** The lathe post's move order is the fact that the law must follow. C is in position before X and Z move, so the work and the chuck have a known angle; the fixed steel has none in the part frame, so it is read at every angle.
- **Pinning tests.** `crates/app/tests/lathe_cross_drill.rs`: `a_cross_drill_over_its_chuck_has_a_clear_first_traverse` (lathe and mill-turn: clear), `a_cross_drill_under_a_wide_tailstock_is_refused_by_name` (lathe and mill-turn: refused), `a_thin_tailstock_centre_is_clear_on_a_lathe_and_convicts_a_mill_turn`. The sim unit tests in `crates/sim/src/verify/live_traverse.rs` pin the slab (the tool width, the station's half-plane, a square bar's face and diagonal, the clipped facet, the spindle line off the origin, the fixed steel read revolved, a start that cannot be read).
- **Known limits.**
  - The law does not use the stated X and Z travel. It reads every height from the band's lowest up, and every radius out. Steel beyond the travel top is read as in the way. That is stricter.
  - The turret itself (its body and the other stations) is not modelled. Only the tool on the station is read.
  - A lathe whose index position is at the low Z end (toward the chuck) is not this premise. The shipped machines and the lathe convention of this project put the free end of the bar at +Z.
  - The other kinds on a mill-turn (milling, axial drilling) still answer to no first-traverse law (`is_mill` is false). That is outside #3077: #3083 owns it.
- **What was considered and dropped**:
  - A new `Warning` variant: it needs mappings in the UI, MCP and the warned moves, and `FirstTraverseLow` says "unknown X/Y", which a lathe does not have. A refusal by name in the cross branch needs none.
  - The work and the chuck read revolved too (the first draft): it refused every legal cross drill on a square bar or a box stock (the corner radius stands over the face's station), 22 tests in the cross-drill suites. The slab reads them at the angle the post states.
  - Vertex samples in the station's half-plane: a large facet can cross the slab with no vertex in it. Each facet is clipped instead.
  - Judging only the mill law on a mill-turn: a mill-turn can post through a lathe post, and the last block goes down at the station's radius.
- **Cost to change later**: low. One sim law (`live_traverse.rs`), one app seam (`operation::cross_traverse`), one call in the cross branch and one flag at the upright judge.
