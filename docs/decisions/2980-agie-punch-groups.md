## #2980 - Independent punch groups on one Agie plate

**Decision.** Accept up to three separately captured modeled punches on one 20 mm setup with the qualified QN2 process. Bind one native work and one ISO resource to each complete source model. Compare each work's full rough-cut, threading and lead envelope with every other work before publishing the bundle.

**Reason.** The control schedules the profile passes for each punch, then its separation passes. The released punches must remain independently supported. A clearance test limited to consecutive works can miss a collision between the first and last punches.

**Alternatives and cost.** A larger count needs qualification of its resource names, native quality records and separation sequence. Mixed die and punch groups need additional checks against the retained die plate. The bounded pairwise comparisons cost at most three checks in the supported range.
